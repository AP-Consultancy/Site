About / Commitment Section
Built to Make Hiring Developers Simple
AP Consultancy exists to remove the friction from technical hiring. We're an IT staff augmentation company that gives businesses direct access to experienced, pre-screened developers — without the long recruitment cycles, the guesswork, or the overhead of full-time hiring.
Every developer we place has been technically vetted, interview-tested, and matched against your actual project needs — not just a keyword match on a resume. Whether you need one developer for six weeks or a full dedicated team for a year-long build, we structure the engagement around your project, not the other way around.
Our commitment to clients:
Speed without shortcuts — fast shortlists, but every candidate is fully vetted first.
Transparency — clear rates, clear availability, no hidden layers.
Continuity — a dedicated account manager stays with you for the life of the engagement.
Flexibility — scale up, scale down, or convert to long-term hires as your project evolves.

We Believe Hiring Great Developers Shouldn't Be Hard
AP Consultancy is an IT staff augmentation company built on one idea: businesses should be able to access great technical talent quickly, transparently, and without unnecessary layers.
Our Story Section
Why We Exist
Technical hiring is broken in two directions — it's either too slow (traditional recruitment cycles that take months) or too risky (unvetted freelancers with no accountability). AP Consultancy was built to solve both problems at once: a pre-vetted bench of developers, ready to deploy fast, backed by a team that stays accountable for the engagement from day one to delivery.
We started by sourcing developer requirements through other staffing vendors. As our bench and delivery track record grew, so did our ambition — today we work directly with hiring companies and are building direct relationships with the large vendor and MSP networks that manage enterprise contingent workforce programs.
What We Believe
Our Beliefs
Speed and quality aren't a trade-off. A 24-hour shortlist only means something if every candidate on it is genuinely qualified.
Relationships outlast placements. We measure success by how long clients stay with us, not just how fast we fill a role.
Transparency builds trust. Clear rates, clear vetting standards, clear communication — no black boxes.
Developers are partners, not resources. We invest in our talent bench because a well-supported developer does better work for you.
